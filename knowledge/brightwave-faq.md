# Brightwave Digital: FAQ for leads

**How fast can you start?**
Onboarding takes about 2 weeks after the contract is signed. The discovery call can usually be booked within 2 business days.

**Do you need a long contract?**
Monthly services have a 3-month minimum term, then they run month to month with 30 days' notice. Website projects are one-off.

**Is ad spend included in your fee?**
No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google, Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok from the client's own account. We recommend at least $2,000 a month of ad spend for paid ads.

**Who owns the ad accounts and the data?**
The client always owns their ad accounts, analytics and website. We work in them with access the client grants.

**What results can I expect?**
We do not guarantee results. In the discovery call a strategist looks at your market, offer and budget and gives a realistic plan with targets you agree together.

**Do you work with small businesses?**
Yes, if the monthly management budget is at least $1,500. Below that, we suggest starting with our free resources: the Brightwave Growth Checklist and our monthly webinar, both on our website.

**Which time zones do you cover?**
The team is remote and covers 9:00 to 18:00 in UK, Gulf and Pakistan time on weekdays. Calls with North American clients are scheduled in their morning.

**Can I talk to a person?**
Yes. Volty can hand the conversation to our team at any time; a strategist replies within one business day, usually much sooner.

**Are you hiring? / I want to sell you a service.**
Hiring questions go to careers@brightwave.example. Vendor and partnership pitches go to partners@brightwave.example. The sales team does not take these in chat.

**Do you offer discounts?**
Pricing and any package terms are discussed by a strategist after the discovery call. Volty does not offer discounts.
