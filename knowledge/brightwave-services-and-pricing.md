# Brightwave Digital: services, pricing and fit

Brightwave Digital is a performance-marketing agency. We work with B2B and B2C companies that already sell a product or service and want more qualified leads or online sales. Team of 18, fully remote, clients in North America, the UK, Europe, the Gulf and South Asia. We work in English.

## Services and starting prices

All prices are monthly management fees in US dollars. Ad spend is paid by the client directly to the ad platform and is NOT included.

| Service | What is included | Starts from (per month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid ads | Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads: setup, creatives, testing, weekly optimisation | $1,500 |
| SEO | Technical audit, on-page fixes, 4 articles a month, link outreach | $1,800 |
| Social media management | 2 platforms, 12 posts a month, community replies on weekdays | $1,500 |
| Content marketing | Blog, email newsletters, lead magnets | $1,200 |
| Website and landing pages | Conversion-focused landing pages or a full site (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify) | One-off project from $4,000 |
| Full-funnel growth package | Paid ads + SEO + content + monthly strategy session | $5,000 |

- The minimum engagement for a monthly service is $1,500 per month.
- Monthly services run on a 3-month minimum term, then month to month with 30 days' notice.
- Recommended minimum ad spend for paid ads: $2,000 per month on top of our fee.
- Exact prices are set by a strategist after a discovery call. Volty must never quote a final price, a discount or custom terms.

## Who we are a good fit for

- Companies with an existing product or service and a website.
- Ecommerce brands, SaaS, professional services (clinics, law, real estate, education), and B2B companies selling to other businesses.
- A monthly marketing budget (fee) of $1,500 or more, and able to start within about 3 months.

## Who we are NOT a fit for

- Pre-launch ideas with no product or budget yet.
- Budgets under $1,500 per month for management.
- Industries we do not take: gambling, adult content, crypto token sales, weapons, tobacco and vaping, unlicensed financial products, "get rich quick" schemes.
- One-off tasks under $1,000 (a single post, logo design, a few boosted posts).
- Political campaigns.

## Process

1. Discovery call (30 minutes, free) with a strategist.
2. Audit and proposal within 5 business days.
3. Onboarding in 2 weeks after signing.
4. Monthly report and a monthly strategy call.

## Things we never promise

- No guaranteed results, rankings, number of leads, ROAS or revenue. Results depend on the market, the offer and the budget.
- No "first page of Google in 30 days" type promises.
- We do not share other clients' names, numbers or results without their permission.
